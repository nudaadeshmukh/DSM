Our Story

Every business depends on a smooth and efficient supply chain, yet managing inventory and transportation is often complex and error-prone. During our academic journey, we observed how small inefficiencies—like overstocking, delayed restocking, or high transportation costs—can significantly impact overall performance. This inspired us to build a system that simplifies these challenges using technology. We started with a simple idea: what if businesses could make smarter decisions using data and algorithms instead of guesswork? With this vision, we developed the Supply Chain Optimization System — a platform that combines concepts like Economic Order Quantity (EOQ), Reorder Point (ROP), and shortest path algorithms to provide practical and intelligent solutions. Through this project, we not only applied our technical skills in web development but also explored real-world problem solving. From designing user-friendly interfaces to implementing optimization algorithms, every step of this journey helped us understand how technology can create meaningful impact. This project represents our effort to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world applications, making supply chain management smarter, faster, and more efficient.
